OUR HERITAGE

GERMAN ENGINEERING
METICULOUS HAND CRAFTSMANSHIP

Headquartered in Sialkot, Pakistan — the global epicentre of medical instrument craftsmanship supplying over 70% of the world's surgical instruments — Xentia Industries merges centuries-old artisanal metalworking heritage with modern German 5-axis CNC machinery and metallurgical science.

Over the past 18+ years, we have forged direct manufacturing partnerships with hospital networks, clinic directors, and private label surgical brands across North America, Western Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.

METALLURGICAL RIGOR

FOUR PILLARS OF PERFECTION

Every instrument manufactured in our facility undergoes calibrated mechanical and metallurgical processing meeting international surgical standards.

1. Raw Metallurgy

Imported AISI 420 German Stainless Steel, Japanese 440C Cobalt Alloy, and micro-grain Tungsten Carbide inserts delivering exceptional edge retention.

2. CNC Micro Machining

Zero-tolerance 5-axis computer numerical control machining for micro-tolerances of ±0.02mm, flawless jaw serrations, and frictionless box joints.

3. Master Hand-Honing

Generational surgical cutlers hand-hone scissor bevels and calibrate forceps alignment under high-magnification optical inspection scopes.

4. Passivation & Autoclave

ASTM A967 nitric acid passivation with ultrasonic cleaning ensures robust chromium oxide protection and 500+ autoclave cycles at 134°C.
