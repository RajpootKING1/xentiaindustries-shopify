PROCUREMENT TRANSPARENCY

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Key information for hospital theater directors, surgical purchasing committees, and medical device distributors regarding our manufacturing standards, custom OEM policies, and global supply terms.

We exclusively utilize certified surgical-grade alloys conforming to ASTM F899 standards: AISI 420 martensitic stainless steel for scissors and cutting forceps (cryogenically heat-treated to Rockwell HRC 52-56), AISI 304/316 austenitic stainless steel for retractor frames and speculums, Japanese 440C cobalt alloy for micro-scissors and shears, and premium micro-grain Tungsten Carbide (HRA 88-90) for needle holder jaws. Certified Mill Test Reports (MTR) and chemical composition spectrographic analyses are provided with every wholesale dispatch.
For initial clinical evaluation and qualification batches, our MOQ is as low as 10 to 25 units per SKU, including custom fiber-laser logo marking. For full-scale custom OEM production (custom dimensional tooling, modified curvature, custom handle knurling), our standard production batches start at 100 units. Turnkey surgical sets can be ordered in quantities starting from a single set.
Yes. Our facility operates high-precision MOPA fiber laser marking stations configured for 2D DataMatrix (GS1-128 and ISO/IEC 16022) serialization. Each instrument can be marked with a unique device identifier (UDI-DI + UDI-PI including LOT and expiration/manufacture date) verified by inline Cognex optical scanners to ensure Grade-A scannability through 500+ autoclave cycles.
Every instrument undergoes chemical passivation adhering to ASTM A967 and ISO 13402 specifications. Following multi-stage ultrasonic de-greasing, instruments undergo nitric/citric acid passivation to enrich the surface chromium oxide layer, followed by boiling water testing (copper sulfate test) to verify zero free iron residue. All instruments are fully certified for routine 134°C (273°F) vacuum autoclave sterilization.
We ship globally across 70+ nations utilizing Incoterms 2020: EXW (Sialkot factory), FOB (Karachi Sea / Sialkot Air), CIF, DAP, and full DDP (Delivered Duty Paid with customs clearance handled directly to your facility door). For urgent sample dispatches and institutional courier shipments, we partner with DHL Express, FedEx Priority, and air freight cargo carriers, providing tracking within 24 hours of dispatch.
For sample purchases and online webstore orders, we accept all major Credit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) via Shopify's PCI Level 1 compliant gateway. For institutional hospital wholesale orders and annual supply agreements, we accept Bank Wire Transfers (Telegraphic Transfer / T/T) and Irrevocable Letters of Credit (L/C at Sight) through prime international commercial banks.
