Sialkot Heritage • Century of Precision Metalworking

Forging Surgical Excellence
At the World's Instrumentation Capital

Merging time-honored metallurgical artisanal hand-craftsmanship with advanced CNC 5-axis machining, metallurgical passivation, and uncompromising ISO standards.

For over a century, Sialkot, Pakistan has been recognized as the indisputable global capital of precision surgical instrumentation, producing upwards of 70% of the world's handheld surgical tools. Generations of metallurgical master craftspeople have refined the art of shaping, hardening, and finishing specialized stainless steels.

Xentia Industries operates directly at the epicenter of this industrial heritage from our dedicated facility on Shahab Pura Road, Small Industries Estate, Sialkot 51310, Punjab, Pakistan. We unite traditional forge-and-anvil expertise with computerized CNC multi-axis milling, vacuum atmospheric furnaces, and cleanroom optical inspection.

The Xentia Manufacturing Mandate "A surgeon's focus belongs entirely on the patient, never on an unreliable instrument. We do not mass-stamp commodity metal. Every instrument is forged, cryogenic-quenched, hand-tuned, passivated, and individually tested to deliver flawless tactile feedback in the operating theater."

Whether providing specialized custom OEM private-label production for international medical device brands or supplying high-volume complete surgical sets for government hospital tenders, Xentia Industries manages the entire production lifecycle from raw bar stock to sterile packaging.
