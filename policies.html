LEGAL & COMPLIANCE FRAMEWORK

INSTITUTIONAL POLICIES

Commercial terms, return warranties, international shipping protocols, and GDPR-compliant privacy practices governing all supply contracts with Xentia Industries.

1. Refund, Return & Cancellation Policy (3-Year Guarantee & Sample Protection)

Xentia Industries maintains a transparent, client-first policy governing clinical evaluations, warranty claims, returns, and order cancellations for institutional hospital networks, surgical distributors, and private practices.

  • 30-Day Clinical Sample Evaluation & 100% Refund: Institutional buyers may evaluate sample batches risk-free. If clinical performance or ergonomics do not match pre-production specifications, return within 30 days for a full refund or immediate precision re-machining.
  • 3-Year Metallurgical Warranty & Free Replacement: Every instrument is guaranteed against defects in materials, heat treatment, and craftsmanship for three (3) years. Any instrument exhibiting premature corrosion under standard 134°C autoclave protocols or micro-fracture during normal surgical use will be replaced immediately free of charge.
  • Order Cancellation Before Dispatch: For standard catalog items, orders cancelled prior to dispatch receive a 100% full refund credited to the original payment method within 5–7 business days. Custom laser-engraved or OEM-tooled batches cancelled after production commencement are subject to raw materials cost reimbursement.
  • Damaged or Non-Conforming Shipments: Any transit-damaged instruments reported within 7 days of delivery with photographic courier verification qualify for priority replacement or instant credit notes without return delay.
  • RMA Protocol: To initiate a warranty claim, refund evaluation, or cancellation, contact our factory desk at info@xentiaindustries.com or WhatsApp +92 349 7400818 with your Order or RFQ reference number.

3. International Shipping & Customs Clearance (Incoterms 2020)

We manage global air and sea freight dispatches to hospital networks and surgical distributors in over 70 nations across North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.

  • Supported Trade Terms: EXW (Sialkot Factory), FOB (Karachi Sea / Sialkot Air), CIF (Named Port of Destination), and DDP (Delivered Duty Paid with customs clearance included for North America and EU).
  • Delivery Partners: Express sample batches are dispatched via DHL Express, FedEx Priority, and UPS Air with direct tracking provided within 24 hours of flight clearance. Bulk containers ship via major ocean lines.
  • Export Documentation: Every commercial dispatch includes a verified SCCI Certificate of Origin, Commercial Invoice, Packing List, and certified Mill Test Spectrographic Report ensuring seamless customs clearance.

4. Terms & Conditions of Supply

All custom OEM manufacturing, private labeling, and bulk production orders are governed by formal sales contracts established between Xentia Industries and the purchasing entity.

  • Custom Engraving & Tooling: Laser logo engravings, custom scale calibrations, and bespoke instrument modifications are fabricated strictly according to buyer-approved engineering drawings. Once sample approval is signed, tooling specifications become binding.
  • Payment Terms: Production runs require a 30% advance deposit upon contract signing, with the 70% balance payable upon final optical QC sign-off prior to dispatch, or via Irrevocable Letter of Credit (L/C at Sight).
  • Lead Times & Scheduling: Standard production runs operate on a 25–45 day manufacturing cycle depending on volume and metallurgical passivations required.

5. Privacy & Data Handling Policy (GDPR / CCPA)

Xentia Industries respects the confidentiality of clinical and institutional procurement officers. We do not sell, license, or distribute client contact information to any third parties.

  • Data Encryption: All transactions and form submissions are transmitted via 256-bit TLS encryption. Payment processing is hosted strictly on Shopify's PCI DSS Level 1 certified platform.
  • Confidentiality: Proprietary surgical instrument blueprints and custom OEM brand identities shared with our sales engineering desk are protected by bilateral Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs).
  • Data Retention & Deletion: Institutional buyers may request immediate extraction or erasure of their account records at any time by contacting our privacy compliance desk.

6. Quality Assurance, ISO 13485 & CE Mark Compliance

Operating from Sialkot, the world's surgical manufacturing capital, Xentia Industries enforces total quality management across raw material sourcing, vacuum heat treatment, and ultrasonic cleaning.

  • ISO 13485:2016 Certified: Comprehensive medical device quality management system certified for precision surgical instrument manufacturing and supply chain traceability.
  • CE Mark Directive 93/42/EEC: All instruments intended for European Union hospitals conform to Class I and Class IIa medical device directives.
  • Raw Material Traceability: Fabricated exclusively from certified German DIN 1.4021 / ASTM F899 martensitic and austenitic stainless steels, complete with chemical analysis certificates.
